# Organizations

An **organisation** is your tenant on the SCO platform. It has its own identity provider, its own projects, and its own isolated set of resources.

---

## One Organisation at a Time

The console signs you in to a single organisation, determined by the organisation identifier you provide when you sign in. To work in a different organisation, sign out and sign back in using that organisation's identifier. See [Accessing the Console](accessing-the-console.md).

---

## What You Manage Within an Organisation

Inside your organisation, the console is where you work with:

- **Projects** — isolated environments for your teams and workloads. See [Projects](projects.md).
- **Organisation users and groups** — the identities and role-based access used across your organisation. These are managed as resources in the console; see [Create an IAM User](../how-to-guides/user/create-iam-user.md) and [Create an IAM Group](../how-to-guides/user/create-iam-group.md).
- **Services** — the virtual machines, clusters, databases, and storage you provision. See [Working with Resources](resources.md).

What you can see and do depends on the access your organisation administrator has granted you.
